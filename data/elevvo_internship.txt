The Elevvo Internship Program

Elevvo Pathways runs a fully sponsored internship programme for students
and early-career developers. Interns choose a track, such as Python
Programming and AI, Data Analytics, or Web Development, and complete a set
of practical tasks that mirror real industry projects.

Interns enrolled in the one-month format must complete at least four tasks
of their choice. Interns in the two-week format must complete at least
three tasks. The tasks are graded by difficulty, from Level 1 fundamentals
to Industry Level projects such as building a retrieval-augmented
generation (RAG) question-answering tool.

The submission deadline for the one-month internship is November 21 at
11:59 PM Cairo time (GMT+2). The two-week internship deadline is
November 5 at 11:59 PM Cairo time. Completed work is submitted through an
official Google Form.

After finishing the required tasks, interns share their work in a public
LinkedIn or Facebook post that tags the Elevvo page. The post should
include a link to the GitHub repository containing the code and a brief
description of the work, or a short screen recording. Certificates are
issued within one week after the internship deadline, once all
requirements have been met. Bonus tasks are not mandatory, but they are
considered during evaluation for badges.
